# Independent analyst and task-benefit protocol

Status: protocol and measurement tools prepared. **No human study or field benefit is reported.**

## Question and scope

Can a coastal-data reviewer resolve and hand off a water-level review with less task time and fewer undocumented decisions than their usual spreadsheet/script workflow? A faster script alone cannot answer this question. Neither flags nor Bitcoin commitments prove that observations are accurate.

## Participants and independence

Recruit consenting people who review environmental time series. Record relevant experience without collecting names in public results. Use anonymous session codes and disclose whether participants helped build Tideprint. A builder running a script is not an independent analyst. Do not contact people or publish their comments without their permission.

Use at least two independently prepared reviews of each study file. Reviewers must decide before seeing one another's results. Export a shared **unreviewed draft** with the same original CSV, source declaration and settings; each reviewer imports a separate copy and completes their own decisions and reasons. They then seal and export receipts. The Compare reviews page revalidates both seals, rejects differing data/settings/source declarations and lists action disagreements. Different reviewer labels do not establish identity or independence.

## Tasks and controls

1. Select representative real station-days and difficult cases before starting. Retain exact raw hashes and source URLs. Mark any deliberately altered inputs. Keep a held-out set; do not tune settings on it after seeing outcomes.
2. Define an identical task: inspect the data, identify issues, document decisions and hand off a checkable record. Include import, learning, review, export and recipient verification time. Also report steady-state time separately if learning is excluded.
3. Split participants between usual-method-first and Tideprint-first order. Use matched different days to reduce recall. Retain both the assignment and deviations.
4. Time actual attempts with the same start/stop rules. Record incomplete attempts, errors, assistance and interruptions. Do not discard slow or failed sessions.
5. Export a measurement note from Compare reviews for each observed task pair. Include the anonymous code, exact task/data reference, both measured times, completion status and issues. The note is self-reported, unsigned and editable; retain original observations and protocol alongside it.
6. Resolve disagreements through a separate domain expert who has not seen tool scores, with reasons and source context. Agreement measures consistency; expert adjudication and uncertainty are needed to discuss error detection.

## Analysis and honest reporting

Report sample size, participant experience, assignment order, all outcomes, paired time differences, median and range. Report the number of completed, partial and failed attempts. Never turn a small convenience sample into a general accuracy claim. Keep separate tables for reviewer agreement, adjudicated detection results, task time and operational performance. If no ground truth is available, say so.

The measurement export allows negative savings: Tideprint can be slower. A positive time difference means minutes observed for that task, not emissions avoided, flood risk reduced or a real-world policy improvement.

## Field and organizational evidence still needed

An environmental-data team must use the workflow in a real review and record who receives the result, whether it changes a decision, what downstream error or time cost was avoided and what uncertainty remains. A sustained pilot should retain incidents, support effort, recovery attempts, network failures, supported volumes and retention/ownership requirements. Repeated real use is stronger evidence than one rehearsed walkthrough.

Tideprint currently supports files up to 3,000 rows, one browser workspace up to 30 saved items and portable backups. It has no shared account system, authenticated reviewer identity, central audit service or global quota. These are deliberate current boundaries. An organization that needs shared approvals or regulated retention requires an additional design and evidence review.

## Evidence package

Retain the predeclared protocol, consent record outside the public repository, anonymous session notes, original inputs, configuration, both receipts, comparison report, adjudication reasons and raw timing records. Publish only data participants and data owners have authorized. No completed study records are included in this repository.
